

# First-Principles Observations of Reality

## What This Repository Is

This repository is a living collection of **first-principles observations** drawn from everyday life.

The entries here are not written as lessons, tutorials, or solved problems.
They are moments where **reality itself revealed a law**, constraint, or mechanism through direct experience.

The goal is simple:

> To capture how fundamental principles of physics, mechanics, electronics, mathematics, and human behavior appear **outside textbooks**, in real time.

---

## Philosophy

Most people encounter scientific laws in classrooms.
They memorize definitions, equations, and diagrams.

This repository works in the opposite direction.

* Start with a **real observation**
* Ask a **naive but honest question**
* Reduce the situation to **first principles**
* Arrive at understanding **without equations unless necessary**

This mirrors how intuition is formed in real scientists and engineers:
through repeated mental simulation of reality.

---

## What You Will Find Here

* Everyday manifestations of **classical physics**
* Mechanical intuition built from ordinary actions
* Electronics and systems behavior seen outside labs
* Childhood observations revisited with mature reasoning
* Mental models that compress complex ideas into simple insights

Each entry is intentionally concise and timestamped to preserve the **rawness of discovery**.
